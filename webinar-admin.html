🛒 Step 5: Checkout Page

When someone clicks "Enroll Now" they need to land somewhere to pay. You have two options:

Option A — Stripe Payment Link SIMPLEST
Create a payment link in Stripe. Free to set up. Stripe sends receipts. You paste the link in the Offer tab. Done.
Option B — Custom Checkout Page
We build a branded checkout page in your Called & Clear theme that embeds Stripe. More professional looking.
🎉 Step 6: Thank You Page

After payment, Stripe redirects them to your Thank You page. We can build this — it would say something like "You're in! Here's how to access your course" with a button or login link. This is where you tell them what to do next.

📚 Step 7: Course Delivery — Where Does the Course Live?

This is the big decision. Where do students actually access the course content?

Kajabi
~$149/mo
All-in-one. Course hosting, emails, landing pages, community. Most popular for coaches.
Teachable
~$39/mo
Simple course hosting. Good for beginners. Handles video, quizzes, certificates.
We Build It

Password-protected HTML course pages hosted on Netlify. Free — but more work to set up.
📧 Step 8: Email Follow-Up (After Purchase)

Once someone buys, you want an automatic email sequence that:
• Sends them their course login / access link
• Welcomes them and sets expectations
• Follows up Day 3, Day 7 to keep them engaged

The easiest tool for this is ConvertKit (now called Kit) — free up to 10,000 subscribers, integrates with Stripe and most course platforms.
